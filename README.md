# HomeWire: Electrical House Wiring and Load Control Board

## Overview

**HomeWire** is a practical electrical house wiring and load control board designed to demonstrate the fundamental principles of domestic electrical wiring. The project provides a simplified model of a household electrical installation, integrating essential components such as **bulbs, switches, a charging socket, a regulator, and electrical piping**.

The primary purpose of this project is to develop a practical understanding of how electrical circuits are installed and controlled in residential buildings. The system demonstrates the connection and operation of different electrical loads through appropriate switches and control devices.

A regulated bulb is used in place of a conventional fan to demonstrate the working principle of a **fan regulator**. By adjusting the regulator, the brightness of the bulb changes, allowing users to observe how voltage or power control affects an electrical load.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* To design and construct a practical electrical house wiring board.
* To understand the basic principles of domestic electrical wiring.
* To demonstrate the operation of switches and electrical loads.
* To integrate multiple household electrical components into a single demonstration board.
* To demonstrate the operation of a charging socket.
* To study the working principle of a regulator using a bulb.
* To observe changes in bulb brightness at different regulator positions.
* To develop practical knowledge of electrical installation and wiring techniques.

## Working Principle

The HomeWire system operates by supplying electrical power to different household loads through properly connected wiring and control switches. Each electrical component is connected according to its required operating configuration.

The **switch box** allows users to control individual electrical loads by opening or closing the corresponding circuit. When a switch is turned ON, the electrical circuit is completed and power is supplied to the connected load. When the switch is turned OFF, the circuit is interrupted and the load stops operating.

The **charging socket** provides an outlet connection for compatible electrical devices. The wiring board also includes a **regulator-controlled bulb**, which is used to demonstrate the function of a speed regulator normally used with a fan. As the regulator position is adjusted, the supplied power to the bulb changes, resulting in observable variations in brightness.

Electrical **piping and wiring channels** are incorporated into the board to demonstrate the physical arrangement and routing of electrical wiring commonly used in household installations.

## Future Improvements

The current wiring board can be further improved by incorporating additional household electrical components such as **circuit breakers, miniature circuit breakers (MCBs), residual current devices (RCDs), indicator lamps, energy meters, and smart switches**.

The system could also be upgraded into a **smart home electrical control system** by integrating microcontrollers, IoT technology, wireless communication, and remote monitoring capabilities.
